Dr. Banerjee is a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Stony Brook University, where he leads the Social & Computational Intelligence Research (SCIRE) Group and is affiliated with the AI Innovation Institute. His research asks how meaning depends on context, and how language technology can remain accountable to it — in discourse, through pragmatic language understanding, computational argumentation, and information integrity; in law, through privacy and regulatory compliance across jurisdictions; and in language communities, through sovereign AI and Indic language processing.

His work has a particular focus on efficient models for pragmatics: designing benchmarks for difficult and overlooked aspects of language understanding, and designing training paradigms that achieve superior results with models 10–100× smaller than contemporary LLMs. This includes the design of task-specific loss functions and careful manifold analysis for problems such as the distillation of small amounts of target data from noisy backgrounds (ACL 2025), the detection of whataboutism in media (ACL Findings 2024), cross-genre misinformation detection (EMNLP 2020), and clinical expert finding (IEEE BIBM 2025). His group also builds open resources, including DNIPRO, a 31-month multilingual and multinational news corpus on the Russo-Ukrainian war (LREC 2026).

His current projects study belief-driven distortion of contraceptive information on social media; privacy law and compliance across jurisdictions, with collaborators at Colorado State University, the New Jersey Institute of Technology, and the University of Calgary; and sovereign AI and Pāṇinian foundations for Indic language processing — a collaboration between the AI Innovation Institute, BharatGen, and the AI Alliance.

His research has been supported by the Society of Family Planning, two awards from the U.S. National Science Foundation Secure and Trustworthy Cyberspace (NSF SaTC) program, the AI Innovation Institute, and Broadridge Financial Solutions, Inc.

He has advised three Ph.D. students to completion, now at Meta (Research Scientist), Boeing AI (NLP Researcher), and Nankai University (tenure-track faculty), and nineteen M.S. and undergraduate students in the group have co-authored peer-reviewed papers.

Dr. Banerjee received his Ph.D. from Stony Brook University, where he received the Computer Science Chair Fellowship. His research on patient-centered identification of adverse drug events received the Best Technical Paper Award at IEEE ICHI (2015). He received his M.Sc. in Computer Science and B.Sc. in Mathematics and Computer Science, both from Chennai Mathematical Institute, where he was the recipient of the National Graduate Scholarship and the National Board of Higher Mathematics Scholarship (both awarded by the Government of India).
