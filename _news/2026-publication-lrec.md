Our multilingual (English, Russian, and Mandarin Chinese), multinational (USA, UK, Ukraine, Russia, and China), and longitudinal (31 months) corpus on the Russo-Ukrainian war is published in **LREC 2026**. &emsp;
[<i class="fas fa-file-pdf"></i>](http://www.lrec-conf.org/proceedings/lrec2026/pdf/2026.lrec2026-1.512.pdf)
<br>
The corpus is dubbed <span style="font-variant: small-caps;">**dnipro**</span> &mdash; Diverse Narratives and International Perspectives on the Russo-Ukrainian Offensive &mdash; and released as a public resource on Zenodo:
<br>
&emsp; &bull; &nbsp; [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20433469](doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20433469)
