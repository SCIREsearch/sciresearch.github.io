Every individual has certain ways of language use that are unique to them (very much like fingerprints).
Such a unique use of grammar and other language features is known as a person's *idoiolect*, which can be used for accurate identification of a text's author &mdash; an important aspect of forensic linguistics. In this line of research, we investigate authorship attribution of various documents, including highly formal technical writings {%cite feng2012characterizing%}, and even collaborative multi-author documents {%cite zuo2019style%}.

<small><b>Note:</b> The project is not our current focus. It sees sporadic progress when there are students interested in pursuing this topic.</small>
