    <span class="ds-badge">DNIPRO</span>
    <h3 class="ds-title">Diverse Narratives and International Perspectives
    on the Russo-Ukrainian Offensive</h3>
  </div>
  <p class="ds-utility">A longitudinal, cross-lingual corpus for studying
  how the same armed conflict is narrated differently across national media
  ecosystems — enabling research in computational journalism, propaganda
  detection, and cross-lingual narrative analysis.</p>
  <div class="ds-stats">
    <span class="ds-pill">246,229 articles</span>
    <span class="ds-pill">3 languages</span>
    <span class="ds-pill">5 nations</span>
    <span class="ds-pill">11 media outlets</span>
    <span class="ds-pill">31 months</span>
  </div>
  <p class="ds-attr">Mohanty, Sabadyn, Rodrigues, Wang, Kalugade, and
  Banerjee &middot; Zenodo &middot; February 2026</p>

  <div class="ds-header">
    <span class="ds-badge">HDCR</span>
    <h3 class="ds-title">Cross-lingual Medical Misinformation
    Detection Dataset</h3>
  </div>
  <p class="ds-utility">A cross-lingual benchmark pairing Chinese health
  claims from news sources with English biomedical evidence across four
  fine-grained distortion types — supporting research in health claim
  verification and medical NLP.</p>
  <div class="ds-stats">
    <span class="ds-pill">72,275 claim-evidence pairs</span>
    <span class="ds-pill">2 languages</span>
    <span class="ds-pill">4 distortion types</span>
  </div>
  <p class="ds-attr">Chaoyuan Zuo (SCIRE alumni) &middot; Zenodo &middot;
  2025 &middot; Paper co-authored with Ritwik Banerjee</p>
