**Tenure-Track Faculty, School of Journalism and Communication, Nankai University**  

Ph.D., Computer Science, Stony Brook University. 2021.

Thesis: Evidence-Based Cross-Genre Misinformation Detection.

Chaoyuan received her B.S. in Computer Science from Beijing Institute of Technology. Her doctoral research comprised ground-breaking innovation on cross-genre detection of misinformation. After defending her thesis in 2022, Chaoyuan accepted an offer from Nankai University, where she is a tenure-track faculty, continuing to innovate in areas related to cross-genre and cross-lingual information retrieval and natural language understanding in healthcare.
